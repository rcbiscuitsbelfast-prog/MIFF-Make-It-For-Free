Welcome to MIFF Studio

Remix a world, inject to preview, or export your pack. Use the Scene Builder to place blocks with Remix Mode core, then export a manifest or send it to the live preview using postMessage.

  1. Choose a world from the selector above and click Open.
  2. Open Remix Mode to place blocks.
  3. Click Inject to Preview to see your changes live.
  4. Click Export to save your remix pack manifest.
